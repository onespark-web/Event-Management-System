Administrative Notes & FAQs

Campus Policies
How are events persisted in this portal?

All events are saved locally inside your browser's persistent storage (localStorage). Edits, removals, and additions survive page refreshes and browser restarts.

Can data be exported for institutional reporting?

Yes, clicking the Export Data button in the top navigation automatically generates a ready-to-use .csv spreadsheet containing all registered events.

What are the validation requirements for new events?

Event IDs must be unique numerical identifiers (100-999). Titles, venues, dates, and capacities are required to ensure data integrity.
